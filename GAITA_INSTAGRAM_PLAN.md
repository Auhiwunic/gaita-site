# Gaita — Instagram Launch Plan

Goal: turn a cold account into app installs, on a $0 budget, using organic reach plus seeded micro-creators. Instagram rewards **saves, shares, and watch-time**, not likes. Every post below is built to earn a save (useful) or a share (relatable), then point to the link in bio.

---

## 1. Account setup (do this first)

**Handle:** @gaita.run (or @getgaita / @gaita.app — pick one that's free, keep it short)

**Name field** (this is searchable, use keywords): `Gaita · Running Form Coach`

**Bio** (no hyphens, punchy):
```
Film one run. See your form like a coach does.
On device. Free. Your video never leaves your phone.
↓ Get it free
```

**Link in bio:** your App Store link (once live) or gaita-site.vercel.app until then. Use a single link, not a Linktree, at launch.

**Profile photo:** the Gaita wordmark on bone or black.

**Highlights** (create 4, covers in brand colors): `How it works` · `The 8 metrics` · `Reviews` · `Free?`

## 2. Content pillars (rotate these)

| Pillar | Why it works | Post types |
|---|---|---|
| **Teach** (form tips) | Runners save + share tips. Biggest reach driver. | Carousels, Reels |
| **Show** (product) | Proof the app is real and looks good. | Carousels, singles |
| **Relate** (runner truths) | "This is so me" = shares. | Reels, singles |
| **Prove** (results/privacy) | Removes the "is this a scam" doubt. | Singles, stories |
| **Founder** (build in public) | People root for a solo builder. | Reels, stories |

Rule of thumb: **teach 2, show 1, relate 1** each week. Never post the product without teaching something around it.

## 3. The launch calendar (3 weeks)

Cadence: **4 to 5 feed posts a week + a story most days + 2 to 3 Reels a week.** Reels drive new followers; carousels drive saves; stories convert your existing followers to installs.

### Week 0 — Pre-launch (build a tiny audience before the app is live)
- **Mon** — Reel: "Something for runners is coming" teaser (film one run clip, no reveal)
- **Wed** — Carousel: *"5 form mistakes stealing your pace"* (pure value, mention app once at the end)
- **Fri** — Reel: founder note, "I built a running coach that fits in one video"
- **Stories:** poll "Do you know your cadence? yes / no idea" — seed the pain

### Week 1 — Launch
- **Mon (launch day)** — Single: *"Now free on the App Store"* + Story countdown + link in bio
- **Tue** — Carousel: *"I filmed one run. Here's the report."* (product demo)
- **Thu** — Reel: "Stop training blind" (fast cuts of the analyze screen)
- **Sat** — Carousel: *"Your watch tracks. Gaita coaches."* (watch vs camera)
- **Stories daily:** link sticker, "get it free", repost any user who downloads

### Week 2 — Educate + proof
- **Mon** — Single: *"Overstriding: the silent pace killer"*
- **Wed** — Carousel: *"The 8 things Gaita reads from one clip"*
- **Fri** — Reel: relatable, "POV: your watch says you ran 'great' but your knees disagree"
- **Sun** — Single: *"Free forever. No catch."* (privacy + on-device angle)
- **Stories:** start reposting seeded creator content (see section 5)

Repeat the Teach/Show/Relate/Prove rotation into week 3+. Reruns of a top carousel as a Reel are fine and often outperform the original.

## 5. Micro-creator seeding (the growth multiplier)

You do not need to pay anyone. The play for a free app is **gifting + affiliate**, not flat fees.

**The offer:**
1. Free lifetime Premium (comp code)
2. An affiliate cut or a unique promo code so their audience gets something and they earn per install/sub
3. A repost of their content on your page (free reach for them)

**Who to target (tiers):**
- **Nano (1k–10k):** highest engagement, will almost always say yes to a free app. Target 20–30 of these. This is your bread and butter.
- **Micro (10k–50k):** a handful. More reach, still reachable by DM.
- Skip anyone over 100k for now (they want money and won't reply).

**Niches that fit Gaita:** running form / drills, run coaches, physios & PTs for runners, "beginner runner" accounts, marathon-training-journey accounts, gait/biomechanics nerds.

**How to find 30 in an afternoon:** search these hashtags and open "recent" + "top": `#runningform #runningdrills #runningcoach #runchat #runnersofinstagram #marathontraining #gaitanalysis #poserunning #runningtips`. Open each poster's profile. Keep anyone with 1k–50k followers, a running-form focus, and comments (real engagement, not just likes). Drop them in the tracker.

**Vetting checklist:** posts in the last 2 weeks · comments look like real people · audience is runners (not other coaches) · content style matches Gaita (form, not just race selfies).

**Outreach:** see `ig/creator-outreach.md` for the DM template and a starter target list. Send 5 a day, personalized. Aim for 30 sent → expect 8–12 yes.

**Compliance:** any creator who posts for you must disclose it. Instagram's "Paid partnership" label or `#ad` in the caption. Put this one line in every agreement.

## 6. What actually moves installs (priorities)

1. **Reels.** They are the only format that reliably reaches non-followers. Even a 7-second clip of the skeleton tracking + a caption hook beats a polished carousel for new reach.
2. **Saves.** Teach posts people save. Saves tell Instagram to show it wider. Every teach carousel should end with "save this for your next run."
3. **Creator seeding.** One nano creator's genuine "this app is actually free and it works" post outperforms 10 of your own posts.
4. **Consistency > perfection.** 5 ok posts a week beats 1 perfect post. The rendered set gives you 3+ weeks of feed so you never miss a day.

Do NOT pay for Instagram/Facebook boosts yet (we covered this). Spend the energy on Reels and DMs.
