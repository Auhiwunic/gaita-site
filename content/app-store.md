## Description (max 4000)
Gaita turns a single side-view phone video into a running-form breakdown — the kind a coach gives you, without the coach.

FILM. ANALYZE. IMPROVE.
Record yourself running (treadmill or track), and Gaita reads your body pose stride by stride to grade the mechanics that actually matter:

• Ground contact time, cadence and overstride — the timing and geometry the camera can truly measure
• Knee and ankle motion across the gait cycle, drawn against a typical range
• Torso lean, shin angle at contact, elbow carriage and foot strike
• Three headline scores — Drive, Impact and Smooth — so you can see progress at a glance

HONEST BY DESIGN.
We only show what a phone can actually measure. No fake force plates, no made-up numbers. Where a metric needs a lab, we say so. As you log more runs, the reference adapts to YOU — comparing your form to your own baseline, not a one-size-fits-all "ideal," because every runner is built differently.

A COACH THAT KNOWS YOUR GAIT.
Ask the AI coach anything about your form or your next workout. It reads your latest analysis and answers in plain language — what's working, what to focus on, which drill to do.

GUIDED RUNS WITH A VOICE IN YOUR EAR.
Start a run and Gaita coaches you out loud — pace cues, mile splits, and encouragement timed to your workout, so you can keep your phone in your pocket.

SHOE WEAR, READ.
Scan your shoe sole and Gaita cross-references the wear pattern with your form to spot overpronation, heel braking and more.

PRIVATE.
Form analysis runs on your device. Your videos are yours.

Form analysis stays free forever. Premium unlocks the AI coach, voice-guided runs, shoe gait scan, and your full analysis history.

Gaita is a training aid, not medical advice. See a professional for pain or injury.
