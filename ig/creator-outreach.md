# Gaita — Micro-creator seeding kit

Goal: get 8 to 12 genuine posts from small running creators, for $0, using a free app + affiliate + reposts. One real "this is actually free and it works" from a nano creator beats ten of your own posts.

---

## The offer (what you DM them)
1. **Free lifetime Premium** (a comp code, once your promo codes are set up in App Store Connect)
2. **A unique code / affiliate link** so their followers get something and they earn per install or sub
3. **A repost** of their content to your page (free reach back to them)

You are NOT paying flat fees at launch. Nano and micro creators say yes to a good free app all the time.

---

## Who to target
- **Nano (1k to 10k followers):** your bread and butter. Highest engagement, almost always say yes. Aim for 20 to 30 of these.
- **Micro (10k to 50k):** a handful, more reach, still reply to DMs.
- **Skip 100k+** for now. They want money and rarely reply.

**Best-fit niches:** running form / drills, run coaches, physios and PTs for runners, beginner-runner journeys, marathon-training accounts, gait / biomechanics nerds. Audience must be *runners*, not other coaches.

---

## How to build a list of 30 in an afternoon
Search each hashtag on Instagram, open **Top** and **Recent**, and open each poster's profile:

`#runningform` `#runningdrills` `#runningcoach` `#runchat` `#runnersofinstagram` `#marathontraining` `#gaitanalysis` `#poserunning` `#runningtips` `#runningphysio`

**Keep anyone who is:** 1k to 50k followers · posted in the last 2 weeks · has real comments (not just likes) · runners as the audience · form-focused content (not just race selfies).

Drop them straight into the tracker below.

## The DM (personalize the first line every time)
> Hey [name], really like your [specific post, e.g. "cadence drill reel"] — the way you break form down is exactly the gap I built for.
>
> I just launched Gaita, a running form coach that grades your stride from one phone video. On device, free, the clip never leaves your phone.
>
> Would love to give you lifetime Premium to try, no strings. If it's useful and you feel like sharing it, I can set you up with a code your followers get a perk with and you earn on. Either way, happy to send it over. Want the link?

Keep it short. Send **5 a day**, not 30 at once (Instagram flags bulk DMs from new accounts). Follow their account first and like a couple of posts before you message.

---

## Compliance (one line, non-negotiable)
Any creator posting for you must disclose it: Instagram's **Paid partnership** label or **#ad** in the caption, even when the only payment is a free app. Put this line in every agreement so you're both covered.
